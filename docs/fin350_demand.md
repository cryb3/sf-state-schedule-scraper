# FIN 350 Student Demand Summary (Last Five Years, Spring/Fall Only)

This report summarizes student demand for FIN 350 offerings, comparing:
- One-day-per-week vs Two-or-more-days-per-week sections
- Morning (start before noon) vs Late afternoon/evening (start at/after noon)

Data window: last ten Spring/Fall terms (approx. five academic years). See the companion CSV for reproducibility and further analysis:

- [`fin350_demand_summary.csv`](../fin350_demand_summary.csv)

## Key Findings

- Across the window, enrollment summed across section types indicates:
  - Two-or-more-days-per-week: 1,377 enrollments over 36 sections (avg ~38.3/section)
  - One-day-per-week: 2,014 enrollments over 56 sections (avg ~36.0/section)
  - Unknown meeting pattern: 555 enrollments over 14 sections
- Time-of-day:
  - Morning (before noon): 40 enrollments over 1 section (insufficient sample; most sections are not scheduled in the morning)
  - Late afternoon/evening (noon or later): 3,351 enrollments over 91 sections (avg ~36.8/section)
  - Unknown: 555 enrollments over 14 sections

> Interpretation: Student demand (enrollment per section) is broadly similar between two-day and one-day formats, with a slight edge toward two-day sections in average fill. The vast majority of demand occurs in noon-or-later schedules; almost no FIN 350 sections (or demand) were observed in true morning slots within the sampling window. For Spring 2026, favoring two-day schedules and afternoon/evening starts is likely to best match historical demand patterns.

## Methodology

- Source: SF State Class Services public class search (FIN subject), Spring/Fall terms in the last five academic years.
- For each term, we fetched the JSON results after priming a session from the results page, then parsed each FIN 350 row:
  - Days-per-week bucket: one unique meeting day vs two-or-more
  - Start time bucket: earliest listed start, classified as morning (< 12:00) or late (≥ 12:00)
  - Demand proxy: total enrollment per section (enrolled) summed by bucket
- Aggregations written to CSV: `fin350_demand_summary.csv` (columns: bucket_type, bucket, sections, total_enrollment)
